degradation, and the progressive shortening of human lifespans from the centuries recorded in prophetic

Chapter 8: Telomeres and the Silencing Operation

they reach a critical length, the cell stops dividing and enters senescence or apoptosis.
The enzyme telomerase can rebuild telomere length. It is active in germ cells and in some stem cells. In most
somatic cells, it is epigenetically silenced.
Within the framework: the telomerase gene exists. The molecular agents composing it have the fitrahprescribed capacity to express the enzyme. The silencing of telomerase in somatic cells is not a neutral
biological feature. It is the output of molecular agents – methyl groups, histone deacetylases, transcription

In earlier human generations – when the franchise’s mode collapse at the molecular level was shallower –

renewal. Longer organismal coherence. Longer life.
The framework does not claim that early humans had different genes. It claims that the same genes, in the

The difference between a thousand-year lifespan and a seventy-year lifespan is not genetic. It is operational.
