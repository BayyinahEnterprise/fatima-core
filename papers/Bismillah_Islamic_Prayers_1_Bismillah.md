Master Cheat Sheet: 100 Invocations
Against Magic, Jinn, and Evil
This specialized document contains 100 powerful protective verses
(Ayat) and supplications (Duas) strictly focused on Ruqyah AshShar'iyyah—the Islamic practice of spiritual defense. Every
invocation is provided with its complete transliteration for direct
recitation, targeted at banishing satans, warding off the evil eye,
breaking sorcery (sihr), and shielding against human and jinn
oppressors.

1. The Ultimate Shield (Al-Fatihah)
Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim. Alhamdulillahi rabbil-'alamin. Arrahmanir-rahim. Maliki yawmid-din. Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka
nasta'in. Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim. Siratal-ladhina an'amta
'alayhim, ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin.
Purpose: The foundational cure (Ruqyah) against all spiritual ailments and magic.

2. The Master Verse of Protection (Ayatul Kursi, Baqarah 2:255)
Allahu la ilaha illa Huwa, Al-Hayyul-Qayyum. La ta'khudhuhu
sinatun wa la nawm, lahu ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-'ard. Man
dhal-ladhi yashfa'u 'indahu illa bi-idhnihi. Ya'lamu ma bayna
aydihim wa ma khalfahum, wa la yuhituna bi shai'im-min 'ilmihi illa
bima sha'a. Wasi'a kursiyuhus-samawati wal-'ard, wa la ya'uduhu
hifzhuhuma, wa Huwal-'Aliyyul-'Azim.
Purpose: Burns away demonic presence and establishes an angelic guard against jinn.

3. The Seals of Al-Baqarah (2:285-286)

Purpose: Destroys the goal of the sorcerer (which is to cause despair) by returning all
