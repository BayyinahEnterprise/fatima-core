Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim

Master Cheat Sheet: 100 Islamic
Protection Invocations (Full
Transliteration)
This expanded document contains 100 powerful protective verses
(Ayat) and supplications (Duas) from the Quran and Sunnah. Every
invocation is provided with its complete transliteration so it can be
recited directly from this document, followed by its purpose for
victory over evil, protection from the Hellfire, the Dajjal, and
comprehensive daily spiritual defense.

1. Al-Fatihah (The Opening)
Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim. Alhamdulillahi rabbil-'alamin. Arrahmanir-rahim. Maliki yawmid-din. Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka
nasta'in. Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim. Siratal-ladhina an'amta
'alayhim, ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin.
Purpose: The greatest Surah, used as a cure (Ruqyah) and fundamental spiritual shield.

2. Ayatul Kursi (Baqarah 2:255)
Allahu la ilaha illa Huwa, Al-Hayyul-Qayyum. La ta'khudhuhu
sinatun wa la nawm, lahu ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-'ard. Man
dhal-ladhi yashfa'u 'indahu illa bi-idhnihi. Ya'lamu ma bayna
aydihim wa ma khalfahum, wa la yuhituna bi shai'im-min 'ilmihi illa
bima sha'a. Wasi'a kursiyuhus-samawati wal-'ard, wa la ya'uduhu
hifzhuhuma, wa Huwal-'Aliyyul-'Azim.
Purpose: The greatest verse for absolute protection; keeps a guardian angel over you at
night.

3. Baqarah 2:285-286
Amanar-rasulu bima unzila ilayhi mir-rabbihi wal-mu'minun... La
yukallifullahu nafsan illa wus'aha... Anta mawlana fansurna 'alalqawmil-kafirin.
Purpose: The last two verses of Baqarah. Reciting them at night suffices against all evil.

4. Al-Ikhlas (112)
Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim. Qul huwallahu ahad. Allahus-samad.
Lam yalid wa lam yulad. Wa lam yakul-lahu kufuwan ahad.
Purpose: Equals one-third of the Quran; ultimate declaration of monotheism against
polytheistic evil.

5. Al-Falaq (113)

6. An-Nas (114)

Ilahin-nas. Min sharril-waswasil-khannas. Alladhi yuwaswisu fi
sudurin-nas. Minal-jinnati wan-nas.
Purpose: The primary shield against internal evils: the whisperings of Satans and men.
