2. WHAT IS THE GOLDEN RATIO?
The golden ratio φ (phi) is a mathematical constant that equals approximately 1.6180339887...
It is the unique solution to the equation: x² = x + 1, which rearranges to x² - x - 1 = 0.
Solution: x = (1 + √5) / 2 = φ
Key Properties:
• φ = 1 + 1/φ (reciprocal relationship)
• φ² = φ + 1 (quadratic property)
• 1/φ = φ - 1 ≈ 0.618 (the inverse is also golden)
• φ appears in the Fibonacci sequence limit
This ratio appears in nature not by accident, but because structures based on φ are mathematically
optimal for energy efficiency, stability, and packing density.

3. THE COSMIC SCALE: GALAXIES

cosmic scale.
